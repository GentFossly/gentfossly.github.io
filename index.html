Working between bloom and motion.

My practice is shaped by flowers, changing light, and the feeling of transformation as a living process. Each work asks how a form can evolve, how movement can be gentle, and how nature can be translated into something sculptural and alive.

Philosophy

I am interested in kinetic art as a way of making time visible. Flowers hold their own language of growth, opening, closing, and returning, and I work with that rhythm to create pieces that feel part botanical study, part moving sculpture. My aim is to make work that feels intimate, organic, and quietly alive.

Practice

My work blends hand-built forms, natural references, and sculptural mechanics to suggest both fragility and force. I am especially drawn to how a single gesture can shift an object from closed to open, still to in motion, and familiar to uncanny.
